What to check before ordering

How do I choose a size?

For shoes, compare your measured foot length with the chart for the specific listing. Check whether the seller measures foot length or the insole. For a hoodie or jacket, lay a garment that fits you flat and compare its chest width, length and sleeve length with the seller’s chart.

What should I check in warehouse photos?

If your agent provides quality check photos, match the color, size label and quantity to your order. Ask for a close-up of visible marks or a photo with a tape measure if the fit depends on a specific dimension. Check the exchange deadline before approving shipment.

How do I compare shipping quotes?

Compare quotes for the same destination and packed parcel size. Ask whether each route uses actual or volumetric weight, whether it rounds up the billable weight, and which fees the quote includes.

Can I buy these items through LitBuy?

Open the CAFANS product page and check its available agents. If you plan to use LitBuy, confirm it can accept the original seller link and the exact variant before placing an order.
